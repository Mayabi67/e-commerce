Why You Should Wear 10–15 mmHg Compression Socks

Compression socks with 10–15 mmHg gentle compression are designed to improve comfort, reduce leg fatigue, and enhance blood circulation. They are perfect for everyday wear and ideal for people who spend long hours standing, sitting, or traveling.

Who Should Use Them?

  • Frequent Travelers: Reduce leg swelling and discomfort during long flights or drives.
  • Athletes and Runners: Enhance muscle recovery, prevent soreness, and improve endurance.
  • Office Workers: Maintain circulation when sitting for long periods.
  • Factory and Healthcare Workers: Reduce leg fatigue during long standing shifts.
  • Pregnant Women: Ease swelling and discomfort from increased leg pressure.

Why Choose 10–15 mmHg Compression?

This light compression level provides all-day comfort without feeling tight or restrictive. It gently supports your veins, improves circulation, and helps prevent tired, achy legs.

Not a Medical Device

These are non-prescription compression socks designed for general wellness and comfort, not for medical treatment. For medical conditions such as varicose veins or lymphedema, please consult a doctor for higher compression grades.
